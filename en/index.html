About

Trained at Instituto del Cine de Madrid. I work across commercials, narrative and music videos.

I see cinematography as part of the storytelling. I like to get involved from prep, work closely with the director and the art department, and find light that makes sense within each scene.

I adapt to each project and each crew, and I care as much about the image as about keeping a good pace on set.

Based in Spain and available for projects in Spain and internationally.
